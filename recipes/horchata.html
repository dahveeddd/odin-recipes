Cinnamon Horchata

Description

Horchata is a traditional Mexican drink made from rice. This is a delicious and versatile drink, whether it is a hot summer day, or if you just want a sweet treat!

Ingredients

  • 10 cups cold water, divided
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 cup almonds
  • 1 (14 ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
  • 6 cups ice

Steps

  1. Bring 4 cups water to a boil. Add cinnamon sticks and let steep for 10 minutes.
  2. Combine rice and almonds in a blender; blend to the consistency of sand. Transfer to a bowl and mix in cinnamon water. Cover and let sit for 6 hours.
  3. Strain mixture through a fine-mesh sieve twice. Save cinnamon sticks but discard almond and rice solids. Add 6 cups cold water, sweetened condensed milk, and cinnamon sticks. Serve over ice.
