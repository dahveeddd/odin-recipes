Authentic Mole Sauce

Description

Traditional Mexican sauce which uses three kinds of dried chiles. This recipe can be made in large batches and freezed, perfect for days when you are in a pinch!

Ingredients

  • 3 dried chipotle chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 2 dried guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 2 dried ancho chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 3 cups chicken broth, divided
  • 1 dinner roll, torn into pieces
  • 2 corn tortillas, cut into 1 inch strips
  • 5 tomatillos, cut in half crosswise
  • 2 tomatoes, cut in half crosswise
  • 1 tablespoon lard
  • 1 onion, halved and thinly sliced
  • 1/2 head garlic, peeled and sliced
  • 1/3 cup chopped peanuts
  • 1/4 cup raisins
  • 2 tablespoons cumin seeds
  • 1 tablespoon dried thyme
  • 6 whole allspice berries
  • 5 whole cloves
  • 3 cinammon sticks
  • 5 ounces dark chocolate,coarsely chopped
  • 3 tablespoons white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Toast chipotle, guajillo, and ancho chiles in a dry pan over medium heat , stirring constantly, until warm and aromatic, about 3 minutes. Transfer to a blender.
  3. Heat 2 cups chicken broth in a saucepan until it begins to simmer, about 5 minutes. Pour broth into the blender.
  4. Toast dinner roll pieces and tortilla strips in a dry pan over medium heat, stirring constantly, until lightly browned, about 3 minutes. Transfer to the blender.
  5. Allow chiles, toasted bread, and tortillas to soak, fully submerged, in the chicken broth until softened, about 10 minutes. Blend mixture until smooth.
  6. Cook tomatillos and tomatoes in a dry skillet on medium-high heat until soft and blackened, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Place tomatoes in the blender with chile puree.
  7. Melt lard in a large skillet over medium heat. Stir in onion, garlic, peanuts, raisins, cumin seeds, thyme, allspice berries, cloves, and cinnamon sticks; cook and stir until onion is soft and golden, 5 to 8 minutes.
  8. Remove cinnamon sticks and other whole spices; add onion mixture to the blender with chile-tomato mixture and blend until smooth.
  9. Pour chile mixture into a large saucepan over medium heat. Stir in remaining 1 cup chicken broth, chocolate, sugar, and salt. Bring to a simmer; stir until chocolate is melted and sauce is thickened and slightly reduced, 10 to 15 minutes.
  10. Enjoy!
